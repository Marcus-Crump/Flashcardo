## Functionality
The idea for this app stemmed from a frustration with using traditional flashcard apps. 
I am a native English speaker who is studying Japanese, and I grew tried of the tedium of having multiple sets of flash cards to learn the word, the def, and the kanji for it. 
I found it to be unnecessarily chaotic for something, like language learning, that is already so. 
I found myself wanting a way to study both the vocabulary and the kanji recognition in one sitting without having to switch physical decks or tabs. <br />
Unfortunately, flashcards with 3 sides, to my knowledge, aren't really a thing, and making 3D prisms to write my terms and definitions on would take up a lot of space.
<br />
These flashcards will be like prisms with each side being a term or a definition. With each card, you will be able to specify the number of faces and the starting face, in addition to all the other typical flashcard application features, or rather the ones I find to be typical.
